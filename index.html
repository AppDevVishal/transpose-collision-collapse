Transpose Collision Collapse

Checks a transpose mapping sheet against three structural rules, then splits colliding mappings onto fresh transpose codes and hands back a corrected workbook.

1. Load a mapping sheet

Drop an .xlsx or .csv here or click to browse. Nothing is uploaded; the file is read in this browser tab.

2. Columns

Three columns are required: Transpose, Target Column and Source Table. The rest are used for reporting and for writing notes back into the sheet when a column exists.

3. The three rules

4. Collapse collisions

Rule 1. Outside the global bucket, no transpose may hold the same Target Column twice. Rule 2. Outside the global bucket, a transpose may draw on one Source Table only. Rule 3. No transpose may be entered on a single row.

Nothing is deleted. Colliding rows are reassigned to unused codes, keeping the largest group on the original code. The tool will not tell you which of two competing definitions is the authoritative one, and it does not repair mapping logic.
